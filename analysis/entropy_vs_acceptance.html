Entropy vs. DFlash2 acceptance length

Muse-Glimmer-30B on SWE-bench Verified agent trajectories — mean accepted draft-block length by target-distribution entropy (final: 50-task SWE-bench Verified run)

Each point aggregates the block-anchor (position 0) Shannon entropy of the target model's full-vocab next-token distribution against how many of that block's up-to-16 drafted tokens DFlash2 accepted, logged directly from vLLM's rejection sampler for every verification step. Low-entropy spans (confident/boilerplate code) get whole blocks accepted; high-entropy spans (uncertain reasoning) get rejected almost immediately — exactly the relationship DFlash's design predicts.
